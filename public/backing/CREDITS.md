# Backing track sample credits

## Drums — `drums/`

From the **Salamander Drumkit** by Alexander Holm
(https://archive.org/details/SalamanderDrumkit), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Modified for KAPPA: overhead takes mixed down to mono, converted from 24-bit to
16-bit, leading silence trimmed, and tails shortened with a fade-out. These
modified samples are likewise released under CC BY-SA 3.0.

## Bass, guitar, piano, organ — `bass/`, `guitar/`, `piano/`, `organ/`

From **tonejs-instruments** by Nicholas Brosowsky
(https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Used unmodified; a
subset of the notes is included.
